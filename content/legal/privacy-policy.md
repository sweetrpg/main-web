# SweetRPG Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Pilgrimage Software ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use SweetRPG, including our websites, apps, APIs, and related services (the "Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It works together with our [Terms of Service](/terms).

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

Pilgrimage Software is the controller of personal data processed through the Service. Contact us about privacy at support@pilgrimagesoftware.com.

## 2. What we collect

**Account data.** When you sign up we receive your email address, a display name, and sign-in identifiers from our authentication provider. If you sign in with a third-party account, we receive the basic profile details that account shares with us. We do not receive your password.

**Profile and community data.** Your username, profile settings, friends list, and the sessions you own or belong to, including your role in each.

**Your content.** Characters, rosters, encounters, game system selections, themes, notes, and other material you create or upload, and the settings of integrations and API credentials you create (we store only a hash of a credential's secret).

**Billing data.** Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive a customer identifier, your plan and purchase history, amounts, dates, and payment status, and limited card details that Stripe shares for display (such as the brand and last four digits). We do not receive or store your full card number.

**Technical data.** Your IP address, browser and device type, the pages and API routes you request, timestamps, and error details. We keep these in server logs and monitoring systems to run, secure, and fix the Service.

**Communications.** Messages you send us for support, and the notices we send you about your account.

## 3. How we use it

- To provide the Service: authenticate you, store and sync your content, run sessions, apply the limits of your plan, and process payments.
- To keep the Service secure: detect abuse, enforce rate limits, investigate incidents, and keep an audit record of administrative actions on accounts.
- To communicate with you about your account, security, billing, and changes to our terms.
- To understand how the Service is used and improve it, using aggregate or de-identified information where we can.
- To meet legal obligations and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

## 4. Legal bases

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on: performing our contract with you (providing the Service); our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, and improving the Service); legal obligations; and your consent, where we ask for it, which you can withdraw at any time.

## 5. Who we share it with

- **Other users, as you choose.** Members of a session you join can see what the session shows to them. Your friends can see the profile information your settings allow.
- **Service providers** who process data for us under agreements: authentication (Auth0), payments (Stripe), hosting and databases (our own servers, and MongoDB Atlas on Amazon Web Services in Oregon, United States), error monitoring (Sentry), email delivery (SendGrid), and logging and monitoring tools we operate.
- **Authorities and advisers**, when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the Service.
- **A successor**, if we merge with or are acquired by another organization, with notice to you.

## 6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and browser storage that the Service needs to work: a sign-in session, security tokens, and your display preferences such as light or dark theme. We do not use advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies or tools. You can block cookies in your browser, but the Service will not work without the sign-in session.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep account data and Your Content while your account is active. When your account is deleted, we delete or de-identify your personal data within 30 days, except for what we must keep for legal, tax, security, or fraud-prevention reasons (for example billing records for 7 years). Server logs are kept for 31 days. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

## 8. Security

We protect personal data with measures such as encrypted connections, access controls, separate databases per service, hashed credentials, and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 9. Your choices and rights

You can see and update most of your profile information in the Service. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to restrict or object to some processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. California residents have rights to know, delete, and correct their data and to opt out of its sale or sharing; we do not sell or share personal data for advertising. To exercise a right, or to ask us to delete your account, contact support@pilgrimagesoftware.com. We may need to verify your identity first and will respond within the time the law requires.

## 10. International transfers

We and our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for transfers out of the EEA and the UK.

## 11. Children

The Service is not for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Each version has a version number and effective date. For material changes we will notify you in the Service or by email at least 30 days before they take effect.

## 13. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: support@pilgrimagesoftware.com.
